Tweet Emotion Labeling Task

Thank you for participating in this study! You will read 5 short tweets, one at a time, and for each one select the emotion you think it expresses.

Instructions

  • You will see one tweet at a time.
  • Choose the single emotion that best describes what the writer of the tweet is feeling: anger, fear, joy, love, sadness, or surprise.
  • There are no right or wrong answers — please go with your first impression.
  • The task should take about 2 minutes to complete.
  • Your responses are recorded anonymously under a random participant ID for research purposes.
